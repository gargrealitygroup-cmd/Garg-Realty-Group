Commercial Plot Due Diligence

What Should You Check Before Buying a Commercial Plot in Dholera?

Commercial property purchases require careful verification. Consider these checks before paying a booking amount.

Verify Commercial Land Use

Confirm whether the specific plot permits your proposed commercial activity under the applicable development plan.

Check Ownership Documents

Review title records, ownership history, encumbrances and any restrictions with an independent property lawyer.

Confirm Exact Plot Location

Match survey numbers, Town Planning Scheme details and final plot boundaries against official records.

Inspect Site Accessibility

Verify lawful road access, site conditions and the current status of nearby infrastructure.

Review Project Permissions

Check relevant development, layout and non-agricultural permissions, along with RERA applicability where required.

Understand Total Costs

Account for the land price, stamp duty, registration, development charges and other applicable expenses.
